Integrate accepted G003 lanes, wire one registered Living Biome project skill, and prove the exact
dry→approved live→receipt chain in a fresh Apprentice scene. Preserve readable skill subgraphs,
three equal-status biome outputs, all source identities, and every pending evidence/human gate.

Begin only after the manifest is `ACCEPTED` with an exact owner motion choice and all four lanes have
clean pushed heads, green component CI, scope-clean diffs, and committed `ready` receipts. Verify
each head against its PR and the frozen base. Merge A→B→C→D using ordinary `--no-ff` commits; never
squash/rebase lane history or redesign a defective lane silently.

## Mandatory live stop conditions

Stop before any action if source is dirty/ambiguous, plan hash changed, selected motion differs,
approval subject/ceiling is stale, current build/license differs, output exists, checkpoint failed,
dependency evidence is not pass, budget is exceeded, cancellation is requested, or a node/parameter
probe differs. Do not broaden approval or replace an artist network.

## Evidence receipt

Record base, lane heads/PRs/merge commits, integration head/PR/final main, exact commands/counts,
Houdini build/license, plan/run/receipt hashes, stage table, graph/data measurements, scene/artifact
paths and live-byte hashes, checkpoint/rollback proof, frame restoration, all approvals, all
pending/not-applicable gates, and any seam repair. Human ratings/winner remain blank until the owner
reviews a stable-order comparison.
