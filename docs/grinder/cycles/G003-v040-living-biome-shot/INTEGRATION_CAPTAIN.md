# Grinder Lane G003-I — Creative Integration Captain

## Mission

Integrate lanes A-D into one readable Living Biome project, execute only explicitly approved graph,
cook, and visual tiers, present authentic animated alternatives for owner critique, and complete one
bounded owner-directed revision. The captain is the sole live-scene integrator and cannot decide
which result is aesthetically best.

## Intake gate

Begin only after:

- Gate V receipt/artifacts passed and were reviewed by the owner;
- H1 records exact artifact hashes, verbatim owner words, and selected/declined continuation;
- the launch record freezes the accepted protected-main base, selected capability/candidates,
  timing, ceilings, worktrees, and lane branches;
- all four component PRs have clean pushed heads, green CI, scope-clean diffs, and ready receipts;
- the integration worktree is clean at the exact frozen base; and
- no sibling lane imported or edited another lane's owned paths.

Verify each lane head against its PR and receipt. Merge A→B→C→D with ordinary `--no-ff` commits;
never squash/rebase worker history or silently repair a defective lane outside its ownership.

## Required integration sequence

1. Rebuild the G002 project plan twice in fresh processes and stop on source/catalog/adapter/hash
   drift. Bind the exact Gate V/H1 identity.
2. Wire the four public APIs through the project skill. Add deliberate cross-lane contract mismatch
   tests and preserve fixed biome order.
3. Run targeted/full pure, Ruff, wheel/package inspection, full H22.0.368 Hython regression, and
   read-only current-build probes. No structural pass grants live or render authority.
4. Produce a no-side-effect graph/data execution manifest: exact new scene/output paths, stages,
   nodes/recipes, seeds, checkpoints, frames, cooks, budgets, cancellation, rollback, and artifact
   expectations. Present it and obtain exact live graph/data approval.
5. After approval, create a fresh project scene, checkpoint before coherent medium-risk edits,
   execute one registered stage at a time, validate graph/data/USD/material/camera/light contracts,
   restore the timeline, save a new `.hipnc`, and hash it. Stop before pixels.
6. Produce a separate visual manifest for the Gate I beauty frames and motion previews. It names
   exact camera, delegate, frames, resolution, samples, time/memory/byte ceilings, output paths, and
   cancellation. Obtain exact viewport/Karma approval.
7. After visual approval, capture authentic pixels and motion. Build individual frames, stable-order
   comparison, playable preview(s), graph views, mechanics reports, and live-byte hashes. Retain
   failed attempts and do not rank the variants.
8. Build Lane D's review packet and present it to the owner. Stop for H2 critique. Bind the owner's
   exact words to exact bytes; do not summarize away uncertainty or infer selection.
9. Use Lane D to propose bounded revision hypotheses. Present the exact chosen hypothesis and new
   graph/cook/render authority. After approval, create non-overwriting R1 scene/artifacts and show a
   stable before/after comparison.
10. Build the final portable creative handoff and receipt, run combined regression, open one
    protected-main PR, merge with history, and verify final-main CI. Release/tag remains separate.

## Live stop conditions

Stop on dirty/ambiguous source, runtime/license/catalog/capability drift, stale approval subject,
output collision, failed checkpoint, unknown node/parameter, missing dependency pass, budget excess,
cancellation, blank/cropped/duplicate sequence, loss of stable order, missing source branch, or any
attempt to auto-fill human taste fields. Do not broaden approval or overwrite an artist network.

## Evidence receipt

Record base, Gate V/H1 identity, lane heads/PRs/merge commits, integration head/PR/final main, exact
commands/counts, runtime/build/license, plan/run/review hashes, stage table, graph/data/USD metrics,
scene and artifact hashes, frame restoration, checkpoints, failures, approvals, H2 exact feedback,
R1 lineage, all pending gates, and continuation instructions for another agent.
