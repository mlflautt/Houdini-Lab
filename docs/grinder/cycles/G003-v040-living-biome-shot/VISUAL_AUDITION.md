# G003 Gate V — Three-Way Motion Visual Audition

## Purpose

Let the owner judge actual motion before selecting a vocabulary for Living Biome. The audition is
not a benchmark and has no automatic winner. It presents three equal-status methods in a neutral,
comparable visual rig while preserving each method's native character.

## Stable presentation order

1. `motion.particle_calligraphy@1.0.0`
2. `generate.differential_growth@1.0.0`
3. `motion.kinetic_reliquary@1.1.0` using its native-only branch

## Neutral presentation contract

- New project-confined scene; never modify or overwrite an artist scene.
- Dark neutral world, one explicit key/fill or dome, one explicit camera, and one neutral readable
  MaterialX treatment per study. Color labels may identify methods but cannot imply rank.
- Exact deterministic seed recorded for each capability. Use registered defaults unless the
  capability would be blank or mechanically unreadable; every deviation is recorded.
- Frame range 1–24. Deliver 12 evenly sampled frames per method in the same temporal order.
- 640×360, Karma CPU, 16 samples maximum. No XPU or third-party renderer.
- Preserve capability-specific scale, but frame each subject with crop-safe margins and a comparable
  screen occupancy target. Do not distort one method merely to make numeric geometry identical.
- Restore the original frame after every bounded cook/render operation.

## Required execution plan

1. Verify clean source, Houdini 22.0.368, Apprentice license, output nonexistence, free disk, and
   registered capability identity.
2. Produce a no-side-effect manifest with exact nodes/recipes, seeds, paths, frames, budgets,
   checkpoint policy, render command, cancellation points, and rollback.
3. Obtain one explicit approval covering only the described graph edits, bounded cooks, 36 total
   output frames, and Karma CPU ceiling.
4. Build each study through registered tools/recipes in a fresh branch or scene; checkpoint before
   medium-risk edits and never consume a human-selected Switch output.
5. Validate graph and sampled geometry before rendering. Stop on drift, warning, budget excess,
   blank output, duplicate frames, cancellation, or checkpoint failure.
6. Render authentic frames, build playable previews and a labeled stable-order contact sheet, run
   deterministic mechanics checks, and hash all durable bytes.
7. Present the artifacts without rankings, recommendations disguised as diagnostics, or prefilled
   selection fields.

## Approval sentence

After the cycle packet is accepted and the exact dry execution manifest is presented, the owner may
authorize the audition with:

```text
Approve G003 Gate V to build three new project-confined motion studies, cook frames 1-24 within the
recorded capability ceilings, and render 12 evenly sampled 640x360 Karma CPU frames per study at no
more than 16 samples, 20 minutes aggregate render time, 4 GiB peak memory, and 1 GiB output; do not
overwrite, install plugins, use external models, select a winner, or continue into Living Biome.
```

The operator must substitute the finalized output root and manifest hash into the approval record.
If the exact manifest changes, request approval again.

## Human review questions

The owner may answer freely; these prompts are optional and non-scoring:

- Which motion has the most compelling sense of life, force, or transformation?
- What feels generic, noisy, stiff, underdeveloped, or visually unclear?
- Which timing moments deserve more emphasis or space?
- Is there a motion you want preserved but not used for Living Biome?
- Do you want one lineage, no lineage, or a specifically described hybrid explored next?

Bind the owner's exact words to the exact artifact hashes. Do not translate them into a winner until
the owner explicitly selects a continuation.
