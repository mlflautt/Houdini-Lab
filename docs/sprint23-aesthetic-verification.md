# Sprint 23 — Staged Reliquary and Perceptual Verification

Sprint 22 proved that the native and MOPs branches are deterministic, editable, bounded, and
visibly distinct over time. Its render is still a diagnostic strip: the point rings are nearly
edge-on, occupy a narrow band, and do not yet read as a composed kinetic artwork. Sprint 23 closes
that gap without pretending that deterministic metrics can decide taste.

## Creative hypothesis

Four camera-facing, layered relic orbits should read as individual kinetic mechanisms while
remaining an equal-status comparison. Outer fragments establish silhouette, counter-rotating inner
orbits establish rhythm, and central cores create focal hierarchy. Warm, cyan, violet, and gold
identities remain labels rather than scores. The graph keeps the verified Sprint 22 packed contracts
upstream and adds presentation only after those contracts.

## Development contract

1. Extend deterministic PNG verification with an explicit panel grid, per-panel fill and margin
   balance, normalized subject centers, and consecutive-frame pixel change.
2. Report mechanical composition warnings such as missing cells, severe margin imbalance, or a
   motion sequence whose sampled frames barely change. Do not emit beauty scores, rankings, or a
   winner.
3. Add a new presentation recipe rather than rewriting the verified Sprint 22 packed branches.
   Face the orbit geometry toward the camera, layer native SOP transforms after each contract, and
   preserve the original candidate IDs and human-only Switch.
4. Add a new Apprentice-safe Karma stage with explicit camera, bounded samples, and render ceiling.
5. Render frames 1, 12, and 24, validate the graph and USD stage, run the grid/sequence analyzer,
   capture graph evidence, and save an incremented `.hipnc` snapshot.

## Acceptance gates

- All 145+ pure-Python tests and Ruff pass on a clean modern Python environment.
- Existing Sprint 22 recipes and packed outputs remain loadable and unchanged.
- Four candidate cells are present with crop-safe margins and recorded fill/center metrics.
- All consecutive sampled frames have non-zero, nonduplicate pixel change.
- The enabled MOPs graph uses only the four already certified MOPs 1.12 node types.
- The MOPs-disabled path creates no plugin nodes and retains the native candidate.
- Geometry, frame, render, and Apprentice limits remain within the declared skill policy.
- `human_rating`, aesthetic winner, and automatic ranking remain empty/disabled.

## Verification boundary

These checks can reject broken framing, absent candidates, static evidence, and extreme imbalance.
They cannot establish that the artwork is beautiful. Final taste remains a human decision; a local
vision-language critic is a later optional advisory rung after calibration, not a Sprint 23
dependency.
